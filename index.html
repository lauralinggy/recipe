Into the Big Blue

The ocean and its deep blue residents can seem intimidating, until you meet them. Beneath the waves, distractions fade, your heartbeat slows, and the blue hugs you like an old friend. I made this guide to help you trade fear for curiosity. The ocean may not feel like home yet, but by the end of this guide, it will. One breath is all it takes to find out. Let's jump in.

What to Bring Into the Blue...(besides confidence)

  • 1 mask
  • 1 snorkel
  • 2 long fins
  • 1 pair of dive socks
  • 1 towel
  • 1 wetsuit (3mm or 5mm)
  • 1 bottle of sunscreen (reef safe)
  • 1 weight belt (with 2 - 4 lbs)
  • 1 handful of snacks
  • 1 trusted dive buddy
  • 1 big breath
  • 2 cups of calm
  • 1 pinch of patience
